VAT AND TAX PREPARATION

Prepared for your accountant, not filed behind your back.

Livyato helps you get organised for VAT and provisional tax. It counts only items you have confirmed, asks for invoices before treating VAT as claimable, and labels everything as an estimate.

Livyato does not file with SARS and is not tax advice. Have a tax practitioner review your figures before you submit anything.

QUESTIONS

Straight answers.

Is my bank statement uploaded to a server?

Not unless you choose. Your file is read in your browser. It is uploaded only if you sign in and choose to save it to your account.

Which files can I use?

CSV and Excel are the most accurate. PDF works when it is text, not a scan or photo. If Livyato cannot tell which columns are which, it asks you to pick them.

Can it file my tax return or VAT201?

No. It prepares organised figures and estimates for you or your accountant to check.

Where is my data kept?

Saved work stays in your browser on that device. Clear it from within the app when you are done, especially on a shared computer.
